Two levels of intelligence

VLA models use a hierarchical architecture: a high-level planner (LLM) decomposes the goal into subtasks. A low-level VLA controller executes each subtask as a stream of motor commands. This mirrors human cognition: "clean the table" is high-level intent; moving arm 3cm left at 0.2m/s is low-level execution. Press Run to see it in action.
